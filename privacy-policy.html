Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications published by JaryqPixel, including:

  • Jigsaw Alive: Jigsaw Puzzles
  • Mosaic Alive: Diamond Painting

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use these applications.

Information We Collect

JaryqPixel does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other information that directly identifies a user.

However, our applications use third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical and device information. This may include:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers
  • Device type, operating system and application version
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Advertising interaction data
  • Application usage and performance information
  • Diagnostic and crash-related information

Advertising

Our applications may display advertising, including banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

Advertising and mediation services may use device information and advertising identifiers to provide, measure, limit and improve advertisements, prevent fraud, and where permitted, provide personalized advertising.

We currently use advertising services that may include:

  • Unity LevelPlay
  • Unity Ads
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads

The availability of individual advertising networks may change over time as part of advertising mediation.

In-App Purchases

Our applications may offer optional in-app purchases, such as virtual currency or removal of certain advertisements.

Payments are processed by Google Play. JaryqPixel does not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Google Play may process information necessary to complete purchases and verify transactions according to Google's own privacy policies.

Game Progress and Local Data

Our applications may store gameplay-related information locally on your device. This may include game progress, completed levels, virtual currency, settings and purchase-related status.

This information is used to provide game functionality and preserve your progress and preferences.

Online Content

The applications may connect to remote servers or cloud storage services to download game content such as images, videos, level information or other resources required by the application.

When connecting to these services, standard technical information such as your IP address may be processed by the hosting provider as part of normal internet communication.

How Information Is Used

Information processed through the applications and third-party services may be used to:

  • Operate and provide application functionality
  • Display and measure advertising
  • Provide optional rewarded advertising
  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Detect fraud and abuse
  • Maintain security
  • Diagnose technical problems and crashes
  • Improve application performance and reliability

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Certain information may be processed by third-party service providers used by the applications, including advertising networks, Google Play services and hosting providers. These companies process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our applications. Information transmitted between the applications and online services is generally transmitted using secure HTTPS connections where supported by the service provider.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Most gameplay information managed directly by the applications is stored locally on the user's device and may be removed by clearing application data or uninstalling the application.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the retention policies of those service providers.

If you have a privacy-related question or request concerning data handled directly by JaryqPixel, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are intended for a general audience and are not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Advertising and other third-party services may apply additional protections where required by applicable laws and platform policies.
