Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JaryqPixel handles information in its mobile applications distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store, including:

  • Jigsaw Alive: Jigsaw Puzzles
  • Mosaic Alive: Diamond Painting

The practices described below may vary depending on the application, platform, installed version, and services available in your region.

1. Information We Collect

Our applications do not require users to create an account or provide personal identification information directly to JaryqPixel in order to play.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we may receive your email address, the information you provide, and any attachments. This information is used to respond to your inquiry.

Our applications use third-party services that may automatically collect or process technical and device information, including:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • Device type, operating system, and application version
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Advertising impressions, interactions, and performance data
  • Application usage and performance information
  • Diagnostic information and crash reports
  • Purchase and subscription-related information

The information collected depends on the services used, platform permissions, device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

2. Advertising

Our applications may display advertisements, including banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and optional rewarded advertisements.

Advertising and mediation partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and advertising interaction data to deliver advertisements, measure their performance, limit repetition, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services.

Where permitted by applicable law and platform policies, advertising partners may also use information for personalized advertising, subject to applicable consent and privacy settings.

Advertising services used by our applications may include:

  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource
  • Unity Ads
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
  • Yandex Advertising Network

The availability and use of individual advertising networks may vary by application, platform, region, and mediation configuration.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Our applications may offer optional in-app purchases, including virtual currency, additional features, advertising-related benefits, and subscriptions.

Purchases are processed by the platform through which the application is distributed:

  • Google Play Billing on Android
  • Apple In-App Purchase on iOS

JaryqPixel does not directly receive or store payment card numbers or banking credentials used to make purchases through these stores.

Google and Apple may process payment information, purchase history, subscription information, and transaction details according to their own privacy policies.

Our applications may process purchase identifiers, transaction status, and entitlement information to deliver purchased content, restore eligible purchases, verify transactions, and prevent duplicate fulfillment or fraud.

Subscriptions are managed through the corresponding Google Play or Apple account. Users can review or cancel subscriptions through their platform's subscription management settings.

4. Personal Photos and User-Selected Content

Jigsaw Alive may allow users to select personal photos from their devices to create custom puzzles.

Access to selected photos is used to provide the requested puzzle creation functionality. This feature is optional and is not required to play puzzles provided by the application.

The application may process the selected image and related puzzle data as necessary to create and maintain the custom puzzle.

Photo access is subject to the permissions and selection mechanisms provided by the operating system.

User-selected photos are not intentionally used by JaryqPixel for advertising purposes.

5. Game Progress and Local Data

Our applications may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Game progress and completed puzzles
  • Virtual currency balances
  • Application settings and preferences
  • Purchase-related status
  • Custom puzzle information, where applicable

This information is used to provide game functionality, restore progress, and preserve user preferences.

Locally stored application data may be removed by uninstalling the application or clearing its data, subject to the operating system's backup and storage behavior.

6. Online Content and Network Connections

Our applications may connect to remote servers, content delivery networks, or cloud storage services to download game resources such as images, videos, level information, and other content required for application functionality.

When connecting to these services, hosting providers may process standard technical information, including IP addresses, request timestamps, and connection-related data.

Such information may be used to deliver content, maintain service availability, detect abuse, and protect network infrastructure.

7. How Information Is Used

Information processed by JaryqPixel and its service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and provide application functionality
  • Maintain game progress and user preferences
  • Download and deliver game content
  • Display, manage, and measure advertising
  • Provide optional rewarded advertisements
  • Process and verify purchases and subscriptions
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and technical problems
  • Maintain application and service security
  • Diagnose crashes and performance issues
  • Improve application stability and reliability
  • Respond to support requests

8. Sharing of Information

JaryqPixel does not independently sell lists of users' personal contact information.

Technical and device information may be processed or shared by third-party services integrated into our applications, including advertising networks, mediation providers, platform purchase services, and hosting providers.

Depending on the user's region, applicable laws, consent, and platform settings, advertising providers may use information for personalized advertising or cross-application advertising measurement.

Third-party providers process information according to their respective privacy policies, contractual obligations, and applicable laws.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through our applications and services.

Network communications generally use HTTPS where supported by the relevant services.

Access to information is limited to what is reasonably necessary for application functionality, support, and service operation.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information managed directly by our applications is primarily stored on the user's device.

Users may remove locally stored application data through available device settings or by uninstalling the application, subject to the operating system's behavior.

Purchase records and subscription information may be retained by Google or Apple according to their respective policies and legal obligations.

Advertising, diagnostic, and technical information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to those providers' retention policies.

Support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

If you wish to request access to or deletion of information handled directly by JaryqPixel, please contact us using the email address below. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

Our applications are intended for a general audience and do not require users to provide their age or create a personal account.

JaryqPixel does not knowingly collect personal identification information directly from children through account registration.

Advertising providers and other third-party services may apply age-related privacy protections where required by applicable laws and platform policies.
